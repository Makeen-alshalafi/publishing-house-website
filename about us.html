About Us

UK Academic Publishing is an international publisher for open access scientific journals in both online and printed versions from London, United Kingdom. Its aim is to serve for the scholars and researchers all over the world by means of fast publishing and to get widest exposure by providing free access. UK Academic Publishing team works with full dedication to improve the services and to maintain high quality standards.

Readers have free and open access to all content.

Our journals are completely open access; research articles, reviews, and other information on this platform are available to everyone for free. To provide open access journals, we support publishing through NO article processing costs.
